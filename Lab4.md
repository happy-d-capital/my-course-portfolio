# Scrambled Eggs

### Ingredients
- Tree Eggs
- Oil or Butter

### Steps
1. put the pan at the stove
2. add oil/butter
3. wait till the pan is hot enough (butter should start **boiling**, and the oil should be very **fluid**)
4. pour the contents of the eggs into a bowl
5. mix the eggs until it has a **uniform yellow color**
6. pour the contents of the bowl onto the pan
7. wait till the eggs are complitely solid with no liquid left
8. the eggs are done and you can put them on a plate.
